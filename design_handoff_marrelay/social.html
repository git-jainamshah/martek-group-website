FAQ · Social

Social questions.

Couldn't find it? Ask on the call →

Which platforms do you cover?

+

Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X are our most common. We focus on the one or two where your audience actually is, rather than spreading thin across all of them.

Do you create the content or just schedule it?

+

We create it, graphics, short video, and copy. If you have a product or founder happy to film quick clips, we'll turn that raw footage into a month of content.

Will it sound like us, not like an agency?

+

That's the whole point. We build a voice guide with you first and check in often early on, so replies and posts feel like they came from your team.

How quickly do you reply to our community?

+

Within an hour during work hours on Manage and Amplify plans. Fast replies are where small brands win, so we treat the inbox as a priority, not an afterthought.
