— Nº 05How we work

A four-act method.

01 — Listen

The question, not the brief.

Two weeks of conversations, audits and quiet observation. We resist solutioning until we understand the actual problem, not the one written down.

02 — Compose

Strategy as a document.

One opinionated point of view — narrative, system, and roadmap — written in prose a CEO can read on a Sunday and a developer can ship from Monday.

03 — Build

Engineered, not assembled.

Small teams, weekly demos, no agencies in agencies. Code in your repo, designs in your file, decisions in writing.

04 — Tend

Software is a garden.

Most relationships continue past launch — performance, content, experiments, and the slow accretion of real product.
