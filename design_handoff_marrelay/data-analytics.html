FAQ · Data

Data questions.

Couldn't find it? Ask on the call →

Which analytics tools do you work with?

+

GA4 and Google Tag Manager for most, plus Mixpanel or PostHog for product analytics, and Looker Studio or Metabase for dashboards. If you're already invested in a stack, we'll work within it.

Our tracking is a mess. Is that a problem?

+

That's the normal starting point, not a dealbreaker. The audit exists exactly for this, we untangle what's there before building anything new.

Is our data kept private?

+

Yes. We work in your accounts with least-privilege access, sign an NDA on day one, and never share or resell anything.

Can you train our team to run it?

+

Absolutely, the setup package includes a training session and documentation so you're not dependent on us. Many clients run it themselves after a month or two.
