FAQ · Web

Web questions.

Couldn't find it? Just ask on the call →

What do you build sites in?

+

Depends on the job. Marketing sites usually run on Webflow or Next.js; product UIs on React + TypeScript; stores on Shopify or headless commerce. We recommend the lightest tool that lets you update things without us.

Do I own the code and design?

+

Completely. You get the repository, the Figma file, and the CMS keys on launch day. No lock-in, no licensing games.

Can you work from our existing brand?

+

Yes, we'll work within your brand guidelines, or build a light system if you don't have one yet. Either way we'll keep it consistent.

What if I need changes after launch?

+

Every full build includes 30 days of free fixes. After that, most clients move to a small monthly retainer, or just email us per-task. No pressure either way.
